Healthcare Case Study for Competitive Intelligence and Monitoring

Competitive Intelligence and Tracking

A Fortune 500 company mandated us to study the competitor landscape of Ultrasound Devices market in Brazil and Mexico.

Some Highlights:

Medical Tourism is likely to increase the market size in border states.

The substantial cost disparities in medical services and ultrasound examinations has resulted in a new category of patients from across the border specially in the border states of Mexico.

The veterinary market emerges as the dark horse!

The veterinary market holds the maximum potential amongst all the other applications and is likely to grow the fastest in Mexico where 70% of houses have at least 1 pet animal as per the national census data of Mexico. The market is highly consolidated with less than 5 key players.
