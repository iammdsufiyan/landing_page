Focused Group Discussions That Deliver Strategic Clarity

At BIS Research, our Focused Group Discussions (FGDs) are designed to extract first-hand market insights by engaging directly with your target decision-makers. Whether you're validating a product concept, analyzing user behavior, or identifying purchase drivers, our expert-led FGDs provide voice-of-the-customer research that delivers strategic clarity and real-world direction. 

How Can BIS Research Help? 
  • Access to 20+ global participant panels for precise, custom recruitment 
  • Physical facility partnerships in over 10 countries for in-person sessions 
  • Expert moderators trained in psychology and behavioral science 
  • Seamless integration with digital diaries and quantitative survey tools 
  • Insight-driven reporting with executive-level readout decks and thematic summaries
