 Frequently Asked Questions 

What is a fundability assessment for deeptech companies?

It evaluates whether a deeptech project has the technical maturity, market evidence, commercial positioning and funding fit required to approach grants, public funding bodies, investors or strategic partners.

Can BIS Research support India RDI Fund readiness?

Yes. BIS Research can help Indian deeptech teams build market-backed evidence around TRL maturity, commercialization potential, strategic alignment, customer use cases and investor readiness.

Can BIS Research support Germany or EU funding readiness?

Yes. BIS Research can help German industrial deeptech companies, applied research organizations and innovation partners build evidence for EU funding readiness and strategic scale-up planning.

Is this only for startups?

No. The service is also relevant for university spinouts, tech transfer offices, incubators, accelerators, investors, AIFs, grant advisory firms, corporate R&D teams and public innovation bodies.

Can BIS Research help with UK grant readiness?

Yes. BIS Research can support UK startups and university spinouts with market validation, competitive intelligence, commercialization evidence and Innovate UK-style readiness inputs.

What is the output of the engagement?

Typical outputs include a TRL assessment, market opportunity assessment, use-case map, competitive landscape, funding pathway assessment, customer map, partner map and fundability scorecard.
