Surgical Procedure Volume Database 

                                    Gain access to in-depth data on global surgical procedure volumes, covering hundreds of diagnostic and therapeutic interventions

Market insights based on data are crucial in the ever-changing healthcare landscape of today.  The Global Surgical Procedure Volumes Dashboard provides the information you need to launch products, penetrate new markets, or improve your approach with confidence.

Key Features:

  • Coverage of 300+ surgical procedures across 13 major specialties (e.g., orthopedics, cardiology, neurology, OB-GYN)
  • Insights from 40+ countries across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, Latin America, and emerging regions
  • Annual updates with multi-year forecasts to track trends and anticipate future demand
  • Detailed segmentation by procedure, region, care setting (inpatient vs. outpatient), and year
  • Interactive dashboard to visualize trends, compare markets, and drill into procedure-level metrics
