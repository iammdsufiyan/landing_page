Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) on Primary Market Research

Q1. What is primary market research?
Primary market research is the process of collecting first-hand data directly from target audiences through surveys, interviews, and focus groups, tailored to your business needs.

Q2. Why is primary market research important?
It helps identify customer needs, market trends, and competitor insights, enabling data-driven strategies and reducing business risks.

Q3. What methods are used in primary research?
Common methods include in-depth interviews, focus groups, KOL interviews, Voice of Customer (VoC) studies, and online/offline surveys.

Q4. How long does a primary market research project take?
Timelines vary by scope—simple surveys may take 1–2 weeks, while multi-country interviews can take 4–8 weeks.

Q5. How much does primary market research cost?
Costs depend on sample size, methodology, and geography. While more expensive than secondary research, it delivers higher accuracy and ROI.

Q6. Can it support market entry strategies?
Yes. Primary research provides real-time insights into customer preferences, competition, and regulations—vital for successful market entry.

Q7. How does BIS Research ensure data quality?
We maintain strict quality checks, GDPR/HIPAA compliance, and connect you with verified global experts for reliable insights.

Q8. Why should companies outsource primary market research?
Outsourcing gives access to hard-to-reach experts, global panels, and faster execution, ensuring credible and scalable insights without straining internal resources.
